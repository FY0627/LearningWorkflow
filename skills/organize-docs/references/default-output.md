# Default Documentation Index

## Filling Notes

- Replace slots with sourced content or explicitly identified gaps and their
  impact; slots supply no default verdict or applicability.
- Add rows for the actual materials, document changes, and follow-up items.
- Preservation entries identify the retained version or independently numbered
  section.
- Describe only documents created or revised in this organizing task.

## Default Markdown Structure

```markdown
# {Documentation index title}

## Scope and sources

- Scope: {included material and work}
- Sources: {material sources and referenced reviews}

## Material index

| Material and purpose | Current document or historical record | Source and revision/candidate | Preservation entry | Evidence availability | Linked gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| {material and purpose} | {record class} | {source and identity} | {entry} | {availability} | {gap reference} |

## Documents created or revised

| Document and entry | Creation or revision |
|---|---|
| {document and location} | {actual change} |

## Unresolved work and handoff

| Gap or action | Responsible role | Follow-up entry | Actual handoff status |
|---|---|---|---|
| {unresolved item} | {role} | {entry} | {status and recipient} |
```
