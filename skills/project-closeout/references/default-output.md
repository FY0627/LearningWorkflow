# Default Closeout Record

## Filling Notes

- Replace slots with sourced content or explicitly identified gaps and their
  impact; slots supply no default verdict or applicability.
- Add one comparison row per applicable completion criterion.
- Keep initial and later outcomes distinguishable and link their record identities.
- Explain record completeness and scoped work achievement separately.

## Default Markdown Structure

```markdown
# {Closeout title}

## Scope, criteria, and candidate

- Closeout scope: {project or phase and included work}
- Applicable completion criteria: {criteria and their source}
- Delivered candidate: {identity and location}

## Criteria comparison

| Completion criterion | Actual result | Judgment | Evidence and source | Remaining action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| {criterion} | {result} | {judgment and basis} | {evidence and provenance} | {action} |

## Initial failures, reminders, and rework

{Initial failures, work completed after reminders, repairs or retests,
and links between the earlier and later records.}

## Separate conclusions

- Closeout record completeness: {judgment, missing information, and impact}
- Scoped work achievement: {judgment against applicable criteria, gaps, and impact}

## Next steps and handoff

| Action | Responsible role | Follow-up entry | Actual handoff status |
|---|---|---|---|
| {next action} | {role} | {entry} | {status and recipient} |
```
